TITLE: Image Quality Assessment: From Error Visibility to Structural Similarity
AUTHORS: Zhou Wang, Alan C. Bovik, Hamid R. Sheikh, Eero P. Simoncelli
PUBLISHED: IEEE Transactions on Image Processing, Vol. 13, No. 4, April 2004
URL: https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2003.819861

ABSTRACT & MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS:
Traditional error summation methods such as MSE and PSNR are based on signal energy differences rather than perceived structural fidelity. The structural similarity index (SSIM) compares local luminance, contrast, and structure:
- Luminance: l(x, y) = (2 * mu_x * mu_y + C1) / (mu_x^2 + mu_y^2 + C1)
- Contrast: c(x, y) = (2 * sigma_x * sigma_y + C2) / (sigma_x^2 + sigma_y^2 + C2)
- Structure: s(x, y) = (sigma_xy + C3) / (sigma_x * sigma_y + C3)
With C1 = (K1 * L)^2, C2 = (K2 * L)^2, K1 = 0.01, K2 = 0.03, L = 255.
Local statistics are computed using an 11x11 circular-symmetric Gaussian weighting function with standard deviation 1.5.

RELEVANCE TO ROUND 3 JUDGE:
- Implemented in `tools/acceptance/metrics.py` as compute_ssim().
- Used in C2 (collateral damage outside dilated mask: SSIM >= 0.98), C3 (inpaint fidelity: SSIM >= 0.90), and C5 (scene text delta SSIM <= 0.02).
