TITLE: Tesseract OCR Engine Architecture and Page Segmentation Modes
AUTHOR: Ray Smith (Google Inc.)
PUBLISHED: ICDAR / Google Research Documentation
URL: https://tesseract-ocr.github.io/tessdoc/

KEY SPECIFICATIONS:
- Line & Word Recognition: Utilizes neural LSTM character line recognizers trained on multi-lingual datasets (vie, eng).
- Page Segmentation Modes (PSM):
  * PSM 3: Fully automatic page segmentation (default).
  * PSM 6: Single uniform block of text.
  * PSM 11: Sparse text: Find as much text as possible in no particular order.
- Stroke & Contrast Enhancement: Tesseract accuracy on degraded or video overlay text improves drastically with CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) and morphological gradient pre-processing.

RELEVANCE TO ROUND 3:
- Powers C1 Residual Overlay Text detector in `tools/acceptance/metrics.py`.
- Uses `--psm 11 --oem 1 -l vie+eng` combined with CLAHE and vowel filters to detect residual characters while suppressing noise.
