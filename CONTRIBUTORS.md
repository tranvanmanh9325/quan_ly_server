## 🤝 How to Become a Recognized Contributor

We welcome community members of all skill levels! When you contribute to this project:

1. **Submit your contribution** via a Pull Request following our [Contributing Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. **Pass all automated checks**: Unit tests, linting, and compile steps in our CI pipeline.
3. **Get your PR merged**: Once your PR is approved and merged into `main`, you will be officially added to this `CONTRIBUTORS.md` file and the `README.md` Hall of Fame!

Thank you for contributing to the future of self-hosted server management and autonomous AI Agents! 🚀
